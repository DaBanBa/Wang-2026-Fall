Overcooked! VR Remake

A multiplayer VR remake of **Overcooked!** where you and your friend can experience the CS student life style in 2026, flipping burgers. Players can grab, throw, and pass ingredients using the controller, then use stations like the cutting board, pan, oven, and flour mixer to prepare flour, water, cheese, beef, tomato, and lettuce.

The main focus is fast-paced teamwork and interaction. Players must divide tasks, interact, prepare ingredients, assemble the burger, and serve completed orders before time runs out, with almost all tasks needing 2 people to interact in some way shape or form.
